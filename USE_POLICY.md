# Use policy

Gizzai Sense is a Gemma 4 derivative. You must follow Google's Gemma Prohibited Use
Policy (https://ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy) and the restrictions in
Section 5 of LICENSE, and pass both on to anyone you redistribute the model to.

Gizzai Sense returns calibrated probabilities, not guarantees. Do not use it to make
fully automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on people:
keep a human in the loop, and route low-confidence answers to a person.
